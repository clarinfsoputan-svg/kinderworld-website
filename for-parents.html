Frequently Asked Questions

What are the school hours and schedule?
Our regular school hours are 8:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday. We also offer extended care from 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM for working parents.
How do you handle parent concerns or questions?
We encourage open communication and are always available to address your concerns. You can schedule meetings with teachers, call the office, or use our parent portal for non-urgent matters.
What is the parent involvement policy?
We welcome and encourage parent involvement through volunteer opportunities, classroom observations, parent education workshops, and participation in school events and celebrations.
How do you communicate student progress?
We provide regular progress reports, hold parent-teacher conferences twice a year, and offer ongoing communication through our digital portfolio system.
