Our Story

Discover the journey that brought Kinderworld Montessori to life, from our humble beginnings to becoming a trusted name in early childhood education across Indonesia.

Our Journey Through Time

2001
The Beginning
Kinderworld Montessori was founded with a vision to bring authentic Montessori education to Indonesia, starting with our first center in Jakarta.
2005
First Graduating Class
Our first group of students graduated, ready for primary school with strong foundations in independence, curiosity, and academic skills.
2010
Expansion to BSD
We opened our second center in BSD, Tangerang, to serve the growing community and bring Montessori education to more families.
2015
Bilingual Program Launch
Introduced our innovative bilingual education program, helping children develop fluency in both English and Indonesian.
2020
Digital Transformation
Enhanced our parent communication and learning tracking systems with modern digital tools while maintaining our core Montessori values.
2024
Today & Beyond
Continuing to innovate and grow, serving over 2,500 families and preparing the next generation of confident, independent learners.
